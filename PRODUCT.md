# AthletiCap Product Context

## Product Purpose
A comprehensive financial and recruitment planning platform for student athletes, transforming recruitment from ad-hoc chaos into data-driven strategy. Athletes track spending, manage coach relationships, compare offers, and predict enrollment outcomes with professional-grade analytics.

## Users
- **Primary:** High school student athletes (ages 15-18) actively recruiting
- **Secondary:** Parents/guardians supporting the process, club coaches advising athletes
- **Tertiary:** Consultants helping athletes with recruitment strategy

## Brand Tone & Voice
- **Authoritative but not intimidating.** Financial planning that feels accessible, not stuffy
- **Clear-eyed.** Cut through recruitment mythology with data
- **Action-oriented.** Every insight should point to a next step
- **Professional.** Respect the athlete's goals; don't patronize

## Strategic Principles
1. **Data drives decisions.** Show numbers, calculations, predictions with transparency
2. **Context matters more than raw metrics.** A 10% conversion rate means nothing without pipeline depth and timeline context
3. **Reduce friction to entry.** First-time athletes should see value in <5 minutes, not after 20-minute setup
4. **Mobile-first thinking, desktop strength.** Athletes are on phones; insights need responsive presentation
5. **Notifications over notifications.** Trigger alerts for real milestones (offer received, coach reply), not vanity metrics

## Anti-References
- **Crypto dashboards** (dark mode excess, neon, gambling aesthetics)
- **SaaS cream** (glass cards, gradients, "hero metric" templates)
- **Spreadsheet clones** (Google Sheets export-obsessed design)
- **Financial scareware** (anxiety-inducing red, alarming language)

## Register
**Product** (design serves the tool, not vice versa)

## Key Features
1. Profile & onboarding (sport, school, academics, budget)
2. Expense tracking (travel, camps, coaching fees, film)
3. Coach contact pipeline (stages, division classification, outreach)
4. School offers (full financial modeling, 4-year projections, appeal letters)
5. School matching (fit score, academic/athletic reach assessment)
6. Enrollment predictions (multi-factor probability by division)
7. Budget advisor (spending benchmarks, category analysis, reallocation)
8. Milestones (sport-specific progress tracking)
9. Dashboard (KPIs, CAC metrics, trends, recent activity)

## Technical Constraints
- Light theme only (user requested)
- DM Sans, DM Serif Display, DM Mono (Google Fonts)
- 2px max border-radius (flat aesthetic)
- In-memory backend for demo (no DB required)
- TanStack Query for data, React Hook Form for inputs
- Minimal shadows, 1px borders, high contrast

## Success Metrics
- New athlete creates profile and logs first 3 expenses in <5 min
- Dashboard gives one actionable insight within 30 sec of visit
- Offer comparison visible without scrolling
- Mobile experience equals desktop experience

## Copy Principles
- Avoid jargon; explain NCAA divisions / offer types inline
- Every button answers "What happens next?"
- Error messages offer recovery paths, not blame
- Numbers always have context (not just "$5000 spent" but "$5000 spent, 50% of budget")
